# Official ratings are fixed by circular; each School may add its own internal ratings

The official learning and conduct ratings that go into the digital report card and Ministry reports come from the Assessment Profile version in force (ADR 0006): formulas, weights, thresholds and level names as the circular sets them, the same for every School and changed only when the circular changes. Each School may also define internal rating schemes of its own (names, thresholds, minimum subject counts, conduct conditions) for awards, class rankings, scholarships or its own programmes; they are shown beside the official result and never replace it on the report card. We rejected letting Schools edit the official thresholds because a signed digital report card is a legal record synced to the Ministry, and a School-edited rule could make it wrong; the internal layer gives Schools the flexibility they asked for without that risk. Internal ratings are built with averages and ratings in phase 1b.
