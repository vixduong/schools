# Phase 1a: build plan

Phase 1a of `docs/roadmap.md` ("ready for the start of term"), cut into slices that each end green (tests pass, `make check` clean) and committed. Built on Phase 0 without changing its model; the standing rules of `docs/phase-0.md` (Constraints sections), `backend/CODING_STANDARDS.md` and `frontend/CODING_STANDARDS.md` apply. Terms per `GLOSSARY.md`; permissions per `docs/roles.md` (the matrix rows for every action below already exist in `backend/permissions/matrix.py`); screens per `DESIGN.md` § Screens.

**Already done in Phase 0**: student and Guardian records, linked (`Student`, `GuardianLink`), Enrollment per year and class with its history, Guardian and student Invitations. The roadmap's first 1a bullet needs no slice.

**Every slice is vertical**: its API, its screen and its tests land together, so the pilot can use each one as it lands.

| # | Slice | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Timetable**: the School's bell schedule (numbered periods per session, morning and afternoon, with times); timetable entries (class × weekday × period → subject, teacher, room) valid from a date inside the school year, so a new timetable replaces the old one from a day without losing it; the teacher must hold a subject-teacher Role Assignment for that subject × class in force; clash checks on class, teacher and room as exclusion constraints; the School admin enters it by hand on a grid per class; the teacher home's "today's periods" reads it | a School admin enters a week for two classes, a teacher or room clash is refused with the clashing entry named, and a teacher sees today's periods |
| 2 | **Attendance**: one mark per student per held period (present, late, absent with leave, absent without leave, plus a note), taken by the subject teacher of that period for the students enrolled in the class on that date; the period counts as taken once saved; changes after the day are audited; the homeroom teacher's overview of the class by day; the teacher home's attendance result per period | a teacher takes a period, the homeroom teacher sees it in the overview, and a teacher of another class is refused |
| 3 | **Leave requests**: a ticket (waiting → approved or declined, or cancelled by the sender while waiting) sent by a Guardian for a linked student, whole days in a date range, with a reason; approved or declined with a comment by the homeroom teacher of the student's class on those days or the principal board; an approved request pre-fills those days' attendance as absent with leave; shown in the teacher home's "Cần cô xử lý" and on the Guardian view | a Guardian's request reaches the homeroom teacher, approval pre-fills attendance, and a Guardian of another student is refused |
| 4 | **Gradebook entry** (Circular 22/2021): periods per year per subject × Grade in the School setup (the count of regular columns depends on it); one gradebook per subject × class × term; columns from the Assessment Profile version in force (regular by periods per year, mid-term, end-of-term; Đạt/Chưa đạt for comment-only subjects); score entry validated against the profile's scale; every change kept as an append-only score history; rows for every student enrolled in the class during the term, with entry refused for dates outside a student's Enrollment; the gradebook screen | a teacher fills a gradebook, a bad score is refused per cell, and the history shows every change |
| 5 | **Gradebook workflow**: draft → submitted → approved (locked) or returned with a comment; the teacher withdraws a submitted gradebook to edit; the group leader of the subject's group or the principal board approves or returns; reopen only by a principal-board member with `can_reopen_gradebooks`, with a reason, audited, edits after it logged; the gradebook's lifecycle chips and red-ink return box; the teacher home's tickets and state board | the full cycle submit → return → resubmit → approve → reopen runs through the API and the screen, each step by the right role only |
| 6 | **Mid-year transfers**: a student who arrives during a term brings results from the previous School, entered by the homeroom teacher with their source School and the transfer document (a file in object storage: `django-storages` S3 backend for R2, the file system in development), shown in the gradebook beside this School's columns and read by the 1b averages; a student who leaves keeps their results here up to the leaving date | an arriving student's carried results and document show in the gradebook, and a left student's row is read only |
| 7 | **Notices**: a notice (title, plain-text body) to an audience the sender may reach (whole School, own group, own class's Guardians, own classes), recipients fixed at sending; read in the app with a read mark; email to recipients who have an email address, sent by a background task (`django-tasks` + `django-tasks-db` per `docs/tech-stack.md`, no Redis) over SMTP, so the provider is a deploy setting | a homeroom teacher's notice reaches the class's Guardians in the app and by email (console backend in tests), and a sender outside the audience is refused |
| 8 | **Guardian and student views**: the phone-first Guardian view of `DESIGN.md` (child switcher; Hôm nay with timetable and attendance; Điểm mới, released scores only; Đơn xin nghỉ; Thông báo) and the student's read-only equivalent without leave requests; installable as a PWA (web app manifest and icons, no offline mode) | a Guardian with two children and a student each see only their own data, on a 375px screen |

Order follows dependencies: attendance needs the timetable; leave requests pre-fill attendance; the workflow needs entry; transfers need the gradebook; the Guardian and student views assemble what slices 1–7 expose. Slices 4 and 5 carry the most risk (no lost or wrong scores is the phase's acceptance bar), so they get an independent review before slice 6.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-04)

- **Score release is per column, by the subject teacher** (slice 4): the teacher publishes a column when it is complete, and only then do the student and their Guardians see it; a later change to a released score shows as changed; approving the gradebook releases every column still unpublished.

## Not in 1a

- Averages, ratings, conduct, report cards and Ministry exports: phase 1b.
- Substitute teachers and one-off timetable changes for a single day; attendance of non-timetabled events; partial-day leave requests; attachments or rich text on notices; Zalo and SMS (phase 5); offline use of the PWA; push notifications.
