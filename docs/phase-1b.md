# Phase 1b: build plan

Phase 1b of `docs/roadmap.md` ("ready for the end of term"), cut into slices that each end green (tests pass, `make check` clean) and committed, in the manner of `docs/phase-1a.md`. Built on Phase 0 and 1a; the one change to a 1a model is additive (slice 1). The standing rules of `docs/phase-0.md` (Constraints), `backend/CODING_STANDARDS.md`, `frontend/CODING_STANDARDS.md` and `docs/slices/README.md` apply. Terms per `GLOSSARY.md` (new terms are added in the slice that needs them); rules per ADR 0006 (profiles versioned by effective date) and ADR 0007 (official and internal ratings); source rules in `docs/research/assessment-regulations.md` and `docs/research/digital-report-card.md`.

**Every slice is vertical**: its API, its screen and its tests land together. One plan per slice in `docs/slices/1b-slice-<n>.md`, written when the slice starts, each ending with a "Files" list and a "Not in this slice" section. A slice plan names the matrix rows it adds (see Permission rows) and updates `docs/roles.md` and `backend/permissions/matrix.py` in the same change.

**Built in 1a and read by 1b**: approved (locked) gradebooks with per-column scores and history, `CarriedResult` rows of arriving students, `AssessmentProfileVersion` with `rules` (schema 1: regular-assessment counts, weights, year average, learning ratings, rounding and `year_average_uses_rounded_semester_averages`), attendance marks per period, the audit log, object storage (`django-storages`), background tasks (`django-tasks`).

| # | Slice | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Averages**: the semester average `(regular sum + 2 × mid + 3 × end) / (n + 5)` and the year average `(S1 + 2 × S2) / 3` (Circular 22/2021) computed in one place from the profile version the gradebook is pinned to, over this School's scores and the student's carried results (a carried score fills an empty cell, so an arriving student can be submitted); comment-only subjects give Đạt/Chưa đạt; additive change to 1a: `CarriedResult` gains a "previous semester average" kind so `(S1 + 2 × S2) / 3` holds for a student who arrives in semester 2, and `gradebook_incomplete` counts a carried cell as filled; averages shown to staff only, provisional while a gradebook is a draft; plan in `docs/slices/1b-slice-1.md` | a fixture of students (full, carried, comment-only, provisional, year from rounded semesters), computed by hand by someone other than the implementer and re-derived by a reviewer, matches to one decimal |
| 2 | **Semester lock**: the principal board locks a semester for a class once every gradebook of that class and term is approved; a locked semester refuses new ratings and score reopenings; unlocking needs `can_reopen_gradebooks`, a reason and is audited | a lock is refused while a gradebook is not approved, a locked semester refuses a conduct change, and unlock leaves an audit row with its reason |
| 3 | **Conduct rating**: the homeroom teacher rates each student of their class per semester (four levels, a comment hidden from the audit log); the year rating derives from the two semesters; after summer training the principal board may enter a named exception with a reason (Circular 22/2021 Art. 13); history kept | a homeroom teacher rates a class, another teacher is refused, a locked semester refuses a change, and the exception is audited with its reason |
| 4 | **Learning ratings, promotion and official awards**: per student per semester and year, the learning rating from the profile version (Tốt, Khá, Đạt, Chưa đạt; the one-step lift applies only when the rating fell **two or more** steps because of a single subject), absence sessions from attendance (default O6), the promotion check, and the official award titles (Học sinh Xuất sắc and Giỏi) from the circular; a verdict of "chưa đạt" shows as **"chờ xét lại"** until re-assessment records exist (O3); a class results screen for the homeroom teacher and the principal board; results calculated on read, frozen at issue (slice 6) | the fixture of slice 1 extended with ratings, lift, absence-limit and award cases matches, and a student without an approved gradebook shows "chưa đủ dữ liệu" instead of a rating |
| 5 | **Internal rating schemes** (ADR 0007): the School admin defines named schemes (name, thresholds on averages, minimum subject counts, conduct conditions) and sees only the scheme, never the results (roles.md "Least data"); the principal board and the homeroom teacher of the class see each scheme's result beside the official rating, labelled as the School's own | a scheme is defined by the School admin, a class shows its result beside the official rating to the principal board, the School admin sees no result, and no scheme changes an official rating |
| 6 | **Digital report card, record and signing**: a record per student per school year assembled from the locked semesters, frozen as a snapshot; the signing chain as a state machine (subject teacher per subject → homeroom teacher → principal → the School's organisational signature), **every signature made through the signing provider** (remote signing, decided 2026-11-26) behind one provider interface; the real adapter lands when the pilot School names its provider, until then a fake adapter in tests; revoke and re-issue instead of edit; each issue logged with time, issuer, purpose and recipient | a class's records run through the chain by the right roles only against the fake provider, a revoked card is replaced by a new issue and the old one stays readable in the log, and a signature step the provider refuses leaves the chain where it was |
| 7 | **Report card files**: XML (shape of the mirrored Decision 3016, marked unverified) and PDF (WeasyPrint) per issued card, built by a background task per class, stored in object storage, downloadable by the right roles; every access and share logged (Circular 70/2026 Art. 16.3) | a class's PDFs are produced as a task and downloaded, the XML validates against the schema in the repo, and each download leaves an access-log row |
| 8 | **Transfer of a report card**: a Guardian consents; the sending School issues a transfer package (XML + PDF of an issued card); the receiving School attaches it to the student as a transfer document, and the homeroom teacher enters the carried results from it through the 1a transfer screen (no automatic import and no read of another School's data) | without consent no package is made, a consented package is attached at School B, and School B reads nothing of School A beyond the file |
| 9 | **Ministry export**: the start-of-year (30/10) and end-of-year (30/6) reports as Excel in the Ministry's import template shape, built as a background task from students, national IDs, enrollment status history and results; the column mapping lives in one data file; the export carries only what the template asks, checked against roles.md "Least data" | the School admin or principal board downloads both reports and every row traces to a student record; **blocked on the template from ticket 12**; the start-of-year report does not read report cards and can ship first |
| 10 | **Guardian and student views for the term's end**: the Điểm mới block adds semester and year averages and ratings once the semester is locked, and an issued report card downloads as PDF for the linked Guardian and the student | a Guardian sees the averages of a locked semester and their child's issued report card, and nobody sees another student's |

Order follows dependencies: ratings need averages and the lock; the report card freezes them; files, transfer and the portal read the issued card; slice 9 waits for ticket 12 and the start-of-year half can move earlier. Slices 1, 4 and 6 carry the risk (a wrong average, rating or signature is the phase's failure), so each gets an independent review of its calculation against the hand-computed fixture before the next slice starts. Slice 6 and 7 may each need splitting at their own plan if it exceeds ten files.

## Permission rows added in 1b

The matrix already holds conduct, the three signatures and the Ministry export. New rows, each added with its slice: lock and unlock a semester (slice 2: principal board; unlock needs the reopen duty); conduct exception after summer training (slice 3: principal board); define internal schemes (slice 5: School admin) and see their results (principal board, homeroom teacher of the class); revoke and re-issue a card, issue a copy to a recipient, build class PDFs (slices 6 and 7: principal board, School admin for the build); consent to a transfer (Guardian) and issue or attach a transfer package (slice 8: principal board issues, homeroom teacher attaches); download an issued report card (slice 10: the linked Guardian and the student, once issued).

## Decisions

Settled by the owner (2026-11-26):
- **Signing**: every personal and organisational signature goes through the School's signing provider (remote signing), not a recorded action in the app.
- **Circular 89/2026** (in force 1 Dec 2026): the new rules apply to school years that start after that date; 2026-27 stays on the version in force on its first day, so no 1a model change. A 2027-28 profile version is added when the circular's text is read; before slice 1 starts, confirm the circular does not require mid-year application.

To confirm with the owner before the slice that needs it:
- **O1, rounding** (slice 1): half-up to one decimal, as schema 1 already states, applied to each semester average once and then to the year average from the rounded semester averages. The research does not state the rule; confirm against the pilot School's current system.
- **O7, carried score fills an empty cell** (slice 1): see `docs/slices/1b-slice-1.md`.
- **O2, timing**: Circular 70/2026 has applied since 8 Oct 2026. If the pilot School must issue before slice 6 lands, it issues on paper for that term.
- **O3, re-assessment** (slice 4): summer re-assessment records and the conduct re-rating after discipline are not built; a failing verdict shows as "chờ xét lại".
- **O5, who may consent to a transfer** (slice 8): the Guardian only; a student of age is not an actor in 1b.
- **O6, absence session** (slice 4): a session is a morning or an afternoon; it counts as an absence when every held period of it is marked absent (excused or not); a late mark does not count. The research says "one session per day" without a mapping.

## Unknowns the Ministry specs settle (ticket 12)

The XML schema (Decision 3016 and its successor), the Excel templates of both reports, the conforming-software list, the signing provider's API and certificate channel for private Schools, and the retention period. The plan assumes: XML follows the mirrored Decision 3016 shape and is marked unverified in code and docs; the Excel layout is a data file; the provider interface has one real adapter, built after the pilot School names its provider. Slices 1 to 5 need none of these.

## Not in 1b

- Primary and Preschool ratings and report cards (phases 2 and 6); averages for Primary never exist.
- Live submission to the national database and API sync; VNeID; a correction-request workflow (Circular 70/2026 Art. 22.8).
- Summer re-assessment records and conduct re-rating after discipline (O3); scholarship workflows beyond showing an internal scheme's result.
- Import of scores from other school systems; a setup wizard; self-serve billing (after the MVP per `docs/roadmap.md`).
- Done for the phase = a pilot School runs the end of a term, and Ministry-shaped Excel exists once ticket 12 delivers the template.
