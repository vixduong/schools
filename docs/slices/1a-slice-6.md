# Phase 1a, slice 6: Mid-year transfers

Slice 6 of `docs/phase-1a.md`. Standing rules: `backend/CODING_STANDARDS.md`, `frontend/CODING_STANDARDS.md`, `docs/slices/README.md`, the Constraints sections of `docs/phase-0.md`, `docs/roles.md` (Rules: Gradebook; Least data), `DESIGN.md` § Screens (Gradebook grid), ADR 0004 (portable storage). Builds on slices 4 and 5: entry, release, the workflow and the grid stay as they are.

Done when an arriving student's carried results and transfer document show in the gradebook beside this School's columns, and a left student's row is read only.

## Terms

**Carried results** (new in `GLOSSARY.md`): the scores a student who arrives during a term brings from the previous School, entered here by the homeroom teacher with the source School and the transfer document. They are never read from another School's data (roadmap), and they stay apart from this School's own entries: no score of this School is ever replaced by one.

An **arrival** is an Enrollment with `reason_started = transfer_in` whose `valid_from` lies inside the term. Carried results exist for the term of the arrival only: the term-1 results of a student who arrives in term 2 have no gradebook of this School to sit beside (the roster is the students enrolled during the term).

## Permission

A new matrix row **Enter carried results of an arriving student** (action `carried_results.enter`): homeroom teacher ✔ own class, everyone else ✘, added to `backend/permissions/matrix.py` and `docs/roles.md` together (`permissions/tests/test_matrix.py` ties them). The sentence "The homeroom teacher sees all subjects of the class but edits none" gains "except an arriving student's carried results". The target is the Student, as of today: the homeroom teacher of the student's class now. Reading the carried results and the document is `scores.view` on the gradebook's subject × class (the gradebook's readers; Guardians and students have no teaching pair, so they never match; slice 8 decides what they see).

## Model (app `grades`)

- **`TransferIn`** (tenant, **append-only**: INSERT and SELECT only; the latest row of an Enrollment is the current one, so a wrong file is corrected by a new row): `student`, `enrollment`, `source_school` (≤ 200), `document` (file, stored under `<school_id>/transfers/<uuid>.<ext>`), `document_name` (≤ 200, the uploaded base name, for display), `recorded_by` (Membership), `recorded_at` (`db_default` `now()`). Composite FKs to student, enrollment and Membership. Check `transferin_source_not_blank`. Trigger `transferin_is_an_arrival` (BEFORE INSERT; the enrollment belongs to the student and has `reason_started = 'transfer_in'`; `FOR SHARE` on it). Who may record is the permission engine's rule, not the database's (the database has no roles; as for every matrix check). The record is writable whatever the gradebook's status (the API does not check it: a document, not a score), but the one dialog saves it together with the results, so on a submitted or approved gradebook the screen disables it; replacing a file there waits for withdraw or reopen.
- **`CarriedResult`** (tenant, **append-only**: INSERT and SELECT only; the latest row of a cell, by id, is its current score, and a row with neither `value` nor `result` clears it): `student`, `term`, `subject`, `kind` (`regular`, `mid_term`, `end_term`), `index`, `value` numeric(3,1), `result` (`pass`, `fail`), `entered_by`, `entered_at` (`db_default` `now()`). Composite FKs to student, term, subject, Membership. Index on `(school, student, term, subject)`.
  - Checks: `carriedresult_value_in_scale` (0 to 10), `carriedresult_result_is_known`, `carriedresult_kind_is_known`, `carriedresult_index_from_1`, `carriedresult_periodic_is_single` (only `regular` has an index above 1).
  - Trigger `carriedresult_student_arrived_in_term`: the student has an arrival inside the term (`FOR SHARE` on the Enrollment, so a concurrent change waits).
  - Trigger `carriedresult_fits_subject`: a scored subject's rows carry `value` or nothing, a comment-only subject's `result` or nothing (reads the subject `FOR SHARE`, as `scoreentry_fits_subject`).
  - Trigger `carriedresult_gradebook_open`: refused unless every gradebook of this term and subject for a class of one of the student's Enrollments overlapping the term is a `draft` (every such gradebook is locked `FOR SHARE` whatever its status, then the statuses are read, so slice 5's step, which locks one `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, conflicts with a save in flight; a filter on `status` before the lock would lock nothing). A submitted or approved gradebook is therefore locked for carried results too (the phase's bar: no changed score under a signed gradebook).
- **Audit**: `TransferIn` is audited (`audit_row_change` on INSERT) with `document` and `document_name` hidden (a file name often carries the student's name; the School admin reads the log). `CarriedResult` is **not** copied into the audit log, for the reason of `ScoreEntry`: the School admin sees no scores; it is its own append-only log with who and when. Reading a document calls `audit.log.record` (a sensitive read).
- **Storage**: `STORAGES["default"]` is `FileSystemStorage` under `MEDIA_ROOT` (development, tests use a temporary directory) unless `R2_BUCKET` is set, then `storages.backends.s3.S3Storage` with `R2_ENDPOINT_URL`, `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` (ADR 0004; dependency `django-storages[s3]`). Files are never served by URL: only through the view below, so the permission check always runs.

## API (under `/api/setup/`)

- `POST gradebooks/{id}/students/{student}/transfer/` (multipart: `source_school`, `document`): `carried_results.enter` on the Student; the student is on the gradebook's roster and has an arrival inside its term (else 400 `{"student": ["not_arrived"]}`). The request is refused by its `Content-Length` before the body is parsed (the parser spools the upload to disk); the document is a PDF, JPEG or PNG by its first bytes (not its name or declared type), at most 10 MB; the stored name is generated, never the client's. 400: `{"source_school": ["required", "too_long"]}`, `{"document": ["required", "too_large", "unsupported_type"]}`. Returns the gradebook detail.
- `PUT gradebooks/{id}/students/{student}/carried/ {results: [{kind, index, value | result}]}`: `carried_results.enter` on the Student; for this gradebook's term and subject. In one transaction, with every gradebook of the term and subject for a class the student was in locked `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` in id order (the set the trigger locks; a class-mover has two), a row is appended for every result that differs from the current one. Errors (400, declared in OpenAPI): `{"results": [{kind, index, code}]}` with `out_of_range`, `too_many_decimals`, `wrong_form` (the subject's form, as for cells), `unknown_column` (a kind and index the gradebook has no column for: carried results mirror the grid's columns; the 1b averages read nothing else), `{"results": ["duplicate"]}`, `{"student": ["not_arrived"]}`, `{"gradebook": ["not_draft"]}`, `{"constraint": [name]}`. No `expected` check: one homeroom teacher owns a class and every change stays in the log (see the last section). Returns the gradebook detail.
- `GET gradebooks/{id}/students/{student}/transfer/document/`: `scores.view` on the pair; the current document of a student on the roster with an arrival in the term, as an attachment with a generated file name (`Content-Type` from the checked type, `nosniff`, `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox`, `Cache-Control: no-store`); 404 when none. Logged with `audit.log.record`.
- `GET gradebooks/{id}/` adds to each student row: `left_on` (the end date of the student's last Enrollment in the class when they are no longer in it, else null), `can_carry` (this member holds `carried_results.enter` on the student, and the student has an arrival in the term), `arrival` (`{arrived_on, source_school, document_name, recorded_at}`, the last three null until recorded; null when the student has no arrival in the term) and `carried` (the current rows: kind, index, value, result).

## Screen

- **Gradebook grid**: an arriving student's name carries the chip "Chuyển đến d/m" and, once recorded, the source School under it with the link "Hồ sơ chuyển" (opens the document). Beside the School's own columns, a column group **Từ trường cũ** shows the carried results (TX, GK, CK as in the headers; ink, never mixed with this School's cells, `tabular-nums`; an empty one "–"). Nothing in the group is editable inline.
- **Nhập kết quả mang sang** (a secondary button on the row, only when `can_carry`): a dialog with Trường cũ, the file (Hồ sơ chuyển; the type and size are said under it) and one field per column of this gradebook, saved with the two endpoints (the file only when one is chosen). A refused field is marked in red ink; the dialog is disabled while the gradebook is not a draft ("Sổ đã nộp, không sửa được").
- **A student who left** (`in_class` false): row greyed, "đã rời lớp từ d/m", read only; the results up to that date stay. This is slice 4's behaviour; the slice adds the date and a test of the leaving case.
- `DESIGN.md` § Screens, Gradebook grid records the above (and the specimen when it shows the grid).

## Known limits

- An Enrollment's start is immutable by the serializer only (`schools/serializers.py`): the owner role could re-date a `transfer_in` row and move an arrival's term. Accepted (phase 0 rule).
- The S3 backend is configured, not run against a real R2 bucket.

## Not in this slice

Importing carried results from a file (the roadmap says "entered or imported"; the 1a row says entered); averages that combine them (1b reads `CarriedResult`); carried results for terms other than the term of the arrival; letting students or Guardians see them (slice 8); an exemption of an arriving student from the columns that closed before they came (a gradebook still needs a score of this School in every column to be submitted, as slice 5; the teacher may enter one for the student, and 1b decides exemptions); deleting an old file when a document is replaced (the replaced one stays in storage until a purge job exists); scanning uploads for malware; a controlled list of source Schools; a conflict check (`expected`) on carried results: a second writer is possible only by changing the homeroom assignment, and every row stays in the log; running against a real R2 bucket (the S3 backend is configured, not exercised).
